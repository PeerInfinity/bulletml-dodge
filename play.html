The bots: the Ace knows every shot before it is fired and never gets hit; the Expert has perfect eyes and reflexes but no foresight, so it plans only from what it sees; the Personality bots play like people, with slower reactions, limited attention and the occasional slip. Pick a personality and a skill (0 = a beginner, 100 = the Expert), or change anything under “Advanced settings”. Each bot can attack or only dodge. The test bots are simple helpers used to check the game. Every game you play is saved when it ends (or on Esc) and can be downloaded and replayed. The original game checks a hit near the back end of each bullet's streak, not at the bullet itself; “centered” moves it to the bullet (white dots show where it is). Press F for a speed overlay.
