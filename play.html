"bot: attack" and "bot: no-attack" are the G4 bot, the Ace (src/game/bot.js), thinking in a Web Worker within a fixed budget per frame (1× fits the 16 ms frame; 4× and 16× may slow the game); "bot: expert …" plans on what it sees (H1); "bot: personality …" is the humanlike bot (H2, src/game/human.js): pick a personality, or set the skill slider (Cautious beginner at 0 → Expert at 100), or change any knob under "advanced" (hover a knob for what it does); its look-ahead is its horizon knob. The others are the G2 test policies. Every game you play is recorded as a tape (the format bin/play-game.mjs writes) when it ends or on Esc.
